LeadLens: AI lead enrichment & scoring for HubSpot

planning

At Robostores, a Brandwise client, qualifying a lead meant checking by hand whether it was a real e-commerce business, what fulfillment it needed, its order volume and whether the contact could decide. LeadLens is the plan to do that research before anyone opens the record: on each new HubSpot contact, Claude researches the company and role, writes a structured summary to the record, and scores it against the ideal customer profile. A person still reviews the score before a lead is routed.

Creative Circuit: automated ad testing pipeline

planning

At Brandwise, every account ran a testing roadmap with success thresholds set before launch, and keeping those roadmaps current across the portfolio was admin work. Creative Circuit is the plan to automate the admin: take a variant matrix (hooks × formats × offers), launch the test cells through the Meta Marketing API, check spend and results against the thresholds fixed in advance, propose pausing the losers, and log every outcome to a running test ledger.

Sentry: spend & anomaly Slack bot

planning

Dashboards are pull; problems need push. At PrimaCare I reviewed pacing every day and watched for creative fatigue by hand, comparing frequency, link CTR and cost per lead week over week. Sentry is the plan to make that push: pull GA4 and ad-platform data daily, run simple anomaly detection on spend, CPA, CTR and conversion rate, and post a plain-English digest to Slack that stays quiet when things are normal and gets loud when they aren't.

Winback Engine: churn-prediction email automation

planning

At PrimaCare, reactivating inactive customers meant building segments by hand from service type, booking history and last appointment. At Food Crowd, a discount-first landing page won more orders but lost revenue per visitor. Winback Engine is the plan to act on both lessons: a SQL and Python model scores each customer's lapse risk from order recency, frequency and category, then pushes risk tiers to the email platform, where offers escalate only as far as the risk justifies the margin cost.

Tagworks: UTM governance & link factory

planning

Attribution dies one rogue link at a time; account audits at Brandwise kept turning up inconsistent traffic-source capture. Tagworks is planned as a Slack slash command that builds campaign links from a locked taxonomy, validating source, medium and campaign values against an allowlist with no free-typing, and logs every link to a central registry. A Chrome extension for the same factory would come later.
