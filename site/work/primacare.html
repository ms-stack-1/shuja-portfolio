PrimaCare: +300% appointments, paid CAC down from $105 to $75

role Performance Marketing Manager (sole marketer) period Jan–Dec 2022 market Dubai · at-home healthcare booking

tl;dr · 30 seconds

Owned the entire marketing function for an at-home healthcare booking platform: Google and Meta at about $15K a month, tracking, landing pages, the booking CRM, lifecycle messaging and reporting. Online appointments grew 300% over 2022. At the same monthly spend, paid-media CAC fell from $105 to $75 between February–March and October–November. A randomized landing-page test raised inquiry conversion from 6.0% to 9.0%, repeat paid bookings per eligible customer rose 37%, and the internal booking CRM I built cut scheduling time 75%.

the_system

Count customers, not clicks

Healthcare bookings fail in the middle of the funnel, and the reporting hid it. Google Ads was counting invalid form submissions as leads, so I reproduced them in GTM Preview, replaced the submit-button trigger with a data-layer event that fires only after a successful submission, and separated form leads from call and WhatsApp clicks. Every lead then entered the WordPress CRM with its source, service, qualification, booking and payment status; returning customers were excluded, and each new paying customer was assigned to one channel under a documented rule.

On that base, acquisition could be judged on cost per paying customer. I ran 5 service-based Google Search campaigns (PCR testing, home nursing, doctor consultations, employer health insurance and home hospital equipment) and service-specific Meta lead campaigns, with bilingual, geo-targeted creative across 200+ Dubai localities. The dashboard showed cheaper Meta inquiries converting worse than Search, so I moved $1,500 a month from Meta to Google without raising the $15,000 total. Comparing February–March with October–November 2022 at about $15K a month in both windows, unique first-time paying customers rose from about 143 to 200 a month and paid-media CAC fell from $105 to $75. That is a before/after comparison of combined changes, not a randomized result.

The booking form got a real experiment. In July 2022 I ran a randomized 50/50 test: a 9-field form against a 5-field form with care details moved to follow-up and a "Book a Free Consultation" call to action. With 2,000 visitors per arm, submissions rose from 6.0% to 9.0% (p ≈ 0.0003), and qualified leads per visitor rose from 4.20% to 5.85%.

The infrastructure made it stick. I built the internal WordPress booking and billing CRM, cutting appointment scheduling time 75%, and a daily-refreshed Looker Studio dashboard joining ad, analytics and CRM data that cut recurring report preparation 75%. Lifecycle follow-up, segmented by service and booking history, ran alongside a 37% increase in repeat paid bookings per eligible existing customer (1.50 to 2.055 across matched 90-day periods); that is a frequency, not a retention rate, and no holdout isolated the messaging.

full_depth · for readers and machines
Execution detail: measurement & attribution
  • Tracking: implemented GTM and GA4 alongside Universal Analytics; Google Ads, GA4 and Meta Pixel events deployed through GTM, with Meta also sending events through the Conversions API with browser/server deduplication.
  • Lead definition: a lead was a successful website or booking submission, or a call or WhatsApp inquiry confirmed in call or CRM records; clicks alone never counted.
  • Attribution: CRM records deduplicated repeat and cross-platform contacts, verified first service and payment, and assigned each new customer to one channel; platform conversions were never summed as unique customers.
  • Rules set before tests: a prespecified primary outcome, comparable traffic allocation where possible, and low-volume comparisons extended rather than declared winners.
Execution detail: paid media, lifecycle & brand
  • Search: end-to-end Google Ads management: keyword research, service-specific ad groups and landing pages, phrase and exact coverage, search-term review, negative keywords, bid management and budget pacing.
  • Social: Meta lead campaigns and remarketing where permitted, testing short video, static images, English and Arabic copy and value propositions, optimized for completed submissions rather than clicks.
  • Scaling rule: a $90 blended CAC target; hold and investigate between $90 and $105, reduce or redirect above $105, and raise budgets 10-15% at a time.
  • Lifecycle: a 24-72 hour follow-up queue for unbooked inquiries, post-service follow-up, and reactivation of customers inactive for 30-90 days, which brought 40 of 500 eligible inactive customers back to a paid appointment.
  • Brand: developed the brand identity and editorial voice from scratch and produced print, web and social content in-house.
