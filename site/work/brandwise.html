the_system

A methodology that scales past one good marketer

Building the practice meant building three things at once. First, the offer: before I joined, the agency sold organic social media management only. I built the service portfolio (SEO, paid search and paid social, retargeting, CRO, web and app development), built the agency's pitch portfolio in Photoshop (it won three new client contracts), and taught business development to pitch and price paid media. Second, the team: I hired 2 marketers into the division and mentored 3 junior marketers on campaign setup, bidding and optimization. Third, the delivery system: a standard onboarding with an audit of each account's tracking and ad setup, testing roadmaps for creative, targeting and funnel structure with success thresholds set before launch, and a performance report for every client covering wins, improvement areas, roadmaps and new campaign ideas.

That structure is what carried the portfolio. By 2022, combined monthly client ad spend was about $272K, roughly 50% Google, 40% Meta and 10% LinkedIn, across 20-25 concurrent paid accounts. Measurement came before scale: I owned the tracking audits, conversion definitions, GTM and Pixel configuration, and from the iOS 14 changes onward moved every client running Meta conversion campaigns to the Conversions API with browser/server deduplication.

I also managed the humans: photographers, designers, writers, SEO specialists and models, briefed and directed to produce the creative the testing program consumed, while I acted as the translation layer between clients, internal teams and external partners.

full_depth · for readers and machines
Execution detail: testing & attribution methodology
  • Testing roadmaps: per-account roadmaps across creative, targeting and funnel structure, with success thresholds set before launch guiding optimization and budget reallocation, so "it looks better" never moved budget.
  • Account audits: six areas checked before launch or restructuring: access and ownership, billing, campaign structure, conversion tracking, website funnels and product feeds.
  • Measurement: each business conversion defined and the end-to-end customer journey tested in GTM Preview, browser developer tools and platform diagnostics before scaling; through the iOS 14 transition, verified client domains, prioritized Purchase or successful Lead events, and moved Meta tracking to the Conversions API with browser/server deduplication.
  • Reporting: a performance report for every client covering wins, improvement areas, roadmaps and new campaign ideas, translated for technical and non-technical stakeholders alike.
