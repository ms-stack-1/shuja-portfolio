tl;dr · 30 seconds

Al Aaraf Jewelers, a Dubai jewelry retailer selling through a showroom and manual Instagram and WhatsApp orders, became a Brandwise client in 2019, and I owned its entire marketing function. I built its first e-commerce store on Magento, set up measurement before media spend, and ran Google and Meta at about $5,000 a month. Average monthly digitally originated revenue rose 134%, from $20,000 to $46,800, paid media returned 5.2x ROAS on attributed revenue, eight target keywords reached Google page one within 6 months, and off-peak showroom visits were up 39% year over year.

Build the store, measure it, then make it earn

Phase one was infrastructure. I built the brand's first Magento storefront over about 4 months for an October 2019 launch: navigation, categories, catalog attributes, price display and imagery for 180 parent products and 320 sellable variants, with my own product photography and Photoshop work. I coordinated the payment and shipping providers and tested checkout transactions; I did not engineer either.

Phase two was measurement, before spend. Google Tag Manager, Universal Analytics Enhanced Ecommerce, the Facebook Pixel and the Merchant Center product feed went in first, and pre-launch QA ran controlled test purchases through every step. After launch I fixed purchase events that fired again when a confirmation page was reloaded, by passing Magento order IDs to Google Ads, and reconciled platform-reported purchases against Magento orders every month.

Phase three was demand. Google Search, Shopping and remarketing plus Meta prospecting and retargeting ran at about $5,000 a month, 40% Google and 60% Meta, and returned 5.2x combined ROAS on attributed revenue in January–March 2020. Technical and category-content SEO took monthly organic clicks from 320 to 1,050 in six months. Geo-targeted English and Arabic promotions ran alongside a 39% year-over-year rise in off-peak showroom visits; that is an observed rise, not proof that the ads caused every extra visit. I also drafted regional expansion plans across MENA, starting with Saudi Arabia and Qatar.

Execution detail
  • Platform: first Magento storefront, 180 parent products and 320 sellable variants, launched October 2019.
  • Measurement: GTM, Universal Analytics Enhanced Ecommerce, Facebook Pixel and Merchant Center feed; pre-launch transaction QA; a fix for duplicate purchase events; monthly reconciliation against Magento order IDs.
  • Paid media: about $5,000 a month. In January–March 2020, Google ran $2,000 a month at 5.0x ROAS and Meta $3,000 a month at about 5.33x.
  • Checkout: a streamlined Magento checkout with guest purchasing, simpler address fields and earlier shipping-cost disclosure. The original test counts can't be recovered, so no lift figure is claimed.
  • SEO: technical and product/category content SEO; eight commercial Dubai jewelry keywords on Google page one within 6 months, tracked in Search Console.
  • Content: CMS, blog, email and social content, plus product photography.
  • Expansion: regional expansion plans across MENA, starting with Saudi Arabia and Qatar.
